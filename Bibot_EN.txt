📖 **Full Description (max 4000 chars)**

**Bibot** — Smart Vietnamese Reading & Storytelling App, your companion anywhere, anytime.

🌟 **KEY FEATURES**

📚 **CUSTOMIZABLE BOOK & STORY LIBRARY**

* **Storytelling:** Hundreds of great Vietnamese stories — folklore, historical tales, novels, and children's books. Read offline with sentence-by-sentence karaoke highlighting.
* **Fairy Tales:** Family-friendly interface, gentle storytelling voice, "Family Friendly" badge. Continuous playback or selective chapter listening.
* **English Books:** Free public domain library (Project Gutenberg, etc.) with natural en-US voice output. Learn vocabulary and practice listening and speaking simultaneously.

🎙️ **VOICE CHAT WITH AI (Using your own AI API)**

* **Real-Talk Voice Chat:** Supports interruptions, quick input, and responsive replies (< 500ms).
* **Offline STT (Vosk):** No internet required, ensuring complete privacy.
* **Multi-engine TTS:** Piper (offline, high quality), Sherpa-ONNX (streaming), and Cloud TTS (Gemini/Google — most natural voices).
* **Custom AI Persona:** Personalize the name, tone, and prompt for "Bi" — your personal AI companion.

🌤️ **DAILY UTILITIES**

* **Weather:** 3-day forecast, feels-like temperature, and rain probability — spoken aloud.
* **News Digest:** Audio updates from top news sources (VnExpress, Tuổi Trẻ, Dân Trí, Google News...). Listen hands-free without looking at the screen.
* **Music Player:** Scans local music library, lets you search and play via default music app or internal player.

⚙️ **ADVANCED FEATURES**

* **Background & Lock Screen Playback:** Foreground service media playback — keeps reading with screen off, fully controllable from the notification bar.
* **Progress Sync:** Automatically saves reading positions (chapter, sentence) to resume instantly upon reopening.
* **Voice Customization:** Adjust speed (0.5–2.0x), sentence pauses, and engine preferences.
* **Offline Models:** Download Piper VI/EN, Vosk STT, and Sherpa Zipformer models for lifetime offline use.
* **Tablet Support:** Responsive grid and landscape Navigation Rail.

💎 **VERSIONS: FREE & VIP**

* **Bibot (Free):** Full access to all features, supported by banner ads, mode-switch interstitial ads (1 per 3 switches), and interval ads (between 16–24 min of reading). Watch a 15-second rewarded ad to unlock 3 premium Cloud voice chapters.
* **Bibot VIP (Commercial):** Ad-free experience, full access to Cloud voices (requires your API key), and priority support.

🔐 **PRIVACY & SAFETY**

* **Fully Offline STT:** Your voice data never leaves your device.
* **No mandatory login**, no personal data collection.
* **Google Play Families Policy Compliant:** Child-directed Fairy Tales section with G rating and disabled personalized ads.

🇻🇳 **TAILORED FOR VIETNAMESE USERS**

* Pure Vietnamese and multilingual interface with full diacritics support (Roboto/Inter fonts).
* Specialized Vietnamese text processing: smart sentence splitting, marker removal, and punctuation normalization.
* Free access to public collections: "Viet Books" and Vietnamese Fairy Tales — instant download without account setup.
